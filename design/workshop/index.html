What we learned

Both products are tools you hardly look at. Their apps are much plainer than their marketing sites, and the apps are what we're learning from.

One typeface in the product

Wispr uses Figtree and Granola uses Melange. Serif shows up only in their marketing headlines.

Wispr Flow, Granola

Paper, and ink that's never pure black

Wispr's canvas is a cream, Granola's an oat. Ink sits at near-black, with two grays under it.

#FFFFEB, #F7F7F2 · ink #1A1A1A, #292929

One accent, kept scarce

Granola's olive appears only on main buttons. Wispr calls its palette "restrained, but bold when needed."

Granola #5B6F00 · Wispr green on calls to action

Hierarchy from ink tone

In Granola, AI-written text is gray and your own text is black. Editing AI text turns it black. Tone changes before size or boxes do.

Granola

A small presence, no spinners

The Flow Bar is one small pill with level bars. Granola floats a pill while recording. The result arriving is the confirmation.

Wispr Flow, Granola

Match complexity to attention

Granola keeps the core notepad sparse and hides advanced tools rather than removing them. For us, that means a near-empty capture moment and a fuller archive.

Specimens

Each foundation on its own. Specimens that depend on a workshop choice follow it.

2.1 Same colors, four grounds

These swatches are identical in all four panels. Compare the middle gray: on cream it tips cool and slightly blue. That's simultaneous contrast, and it's why canvas warmth matters more for Palette than for a notes app.

2.2 Ink on every ground

Primary and secondary ink must reach 4.5:1 wherever they sit. Tertiary is only for non-text unless it passes. The ratios follow the theme and canvas you picked.

2.4 and 2.6 Type scale

Four sizes and two weights, in the typeface you picked. Section labels move from 11 pt tracked caps to 13 pt medium in secondary ink.

2.5 Values without mono

Tabular figures give every digit the same width, so columns of values line up the way mono did, without a second typeface.
