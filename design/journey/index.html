Bergen Street, golden hour

Walk 14, late September.

412 Bergen Street.

A little before seven the sun slides behind the row houses across the street, and for ten minutes every stoop on the block turns the same warm brick. The doors hold out. 412 keeps its teal all the way into the shadow.
