Voiceover script

Play the guide: the film with no sound, and each line under the picture as it plays.

How to record

  1. Find a quiet room with soft things around you. A closet full of clothes works well.
  2. Hold the phone a hand's width from your mouth, a little to one side, and keep it there.
  3. Start each recording with three seconds of silence. The cleaner learns the room from it.
  4. Say each line two or three times, with a breath between takes.
  5. Save one file per line, named by its id (for example, brief-1), and send them to Noah.
  6. Play the guide as you read: it shows each line under the picture as it plays in the film. Say it at your own pace. The picture is re-timed to your takes.
