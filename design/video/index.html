A loose script and blocking for about eight minutes, in Naheel's shape: the problem, the product working end to end, then three deep dives (the look, the user, the story) that show the approaches we tried, where we landed and why, with the research's numbers on screen. Every line is a draft. Click a line to rewrite it, click a speaker to switch who says it, and press “I'm done” when you want Claude to fold your edits in. Each section opens on a statement, a white screen with one word in one of our colors: the lines are placeholders for you to write, and clicking a word colors it. Each section lasts as long as its lines take to say, a little faster than a normal pace (about 2.8 words a second), and the picture keeps pace with you: cut a line and its section gets shorter, add one and the picture waits.
